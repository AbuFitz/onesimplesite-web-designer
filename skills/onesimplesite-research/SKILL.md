# OneSimpleSite Research

Produce decision-ready evidence for a website without turning plausible findings into client facts.

## Frame the research

State which site decisions the research must support. Use only relevant lanes: business truth, audience questions, market patterns, reference translation, search language, local context, technical constraints, and integration requirements.

Start from supplied materials. For external research, prefer primary sources and current official documentation. Open the source behind search results. Record publisher, URL, date or last update when available, access date, and whether each conclusion is direct evidence or inference.

## Maintain a fact ledger

Classify every consequential item:

- `VERIFIED`: supported by client material or an authoritative source;
- `INFERRED`: a reasonable but reversible conclusion;
- `PROVISIONAL`: draft material requiring client approval;
- `CONFLICTED`: credible sources disagree;
- `BLOCKED`: missing information prevents a responsible decision.

Never use a competitor, directory, AI summary, or review site as proof of the client's credentials, price, availability, performance, or customer claims. Corroborate consequential facts when practical.

## Analyse rather than collect

Inspect a small representative market set. Compare promise, CTA, proof, information order, tone, typography, imagery, motion, mobile behavior, accessibility, and technical friction. Separate category conventions users rely on from repeated visual habits the new site can avoid.

Translate reference sites into observable attributes. Do not copy identity, wording, proprietary imagery, illustrations, or near-identical composition.

## Output

Return:

1. the decision questions;
2. a source list;
3. the fact ledger;
4. audience and market patterns;
5. implications for content, conversion, art direction, architecture, and SEO;
6. blockers and client questions;
7. a concise evidence-backed website brief.

Write conclusions by decision, not one summary per source. Cite any claim derived from the web. State uncertainty plainly.
