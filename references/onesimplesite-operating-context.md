# OneSimpleSite operating context

Read this when the request concerns the OneSimpleSite business, canonical repository, suite architecture, roadmap, or how the skills fit together. Ordinary client website builds do not need it.

## Canonical source

Repository: https://github.com/AbuFitz/onesimplesite-web-designer

The repository is the source of truth for the portable Claude Code and Codex suite, supporting references, font catalogue, evaluation prompts, installers, and OneSimpleSite showcase.

## Business and product intent

OneSimpleSite is a web-design and build system capable of both ends of the market:

- commercially clear, distinctive sites for everyday customer businesses;
- high-concept editorial and immersive sites that can use 3D, WebGL, canvas, generative design, spatial type, and authored motion.

The goal is not to force every client into a recognisable visual template. The repeatable product is the reasoning standard: evidence, buyer logic, original art direction, complete implementation, responsive accessibility, performance restraint, and browser verification.

## Centralisation rule

Keep ordinary specialist knowledge as references routed by the main skill. Maintain separate companion skills only when they have a meaningfully independent trigger:

- `onesimplesite-research` for research-only discovery and a sourced fact ledger;
- `onesimplesite-skill-lab` for evaluating and improving the suite itself.

Do not fragment the suite into dozens of overlapping skills merely because a topic has depth.

## Truth and engineering standard

Use verified, inferred, provisional, and blocking states. Do not turn plausible copy into facts. Expose consequential uncertainty. Prefer the simplest capable stack, preserve existing repositories surgically, create a runnable spine early, and define observable completion criteria.

For visual work, derive one creative thesis from the subject. Treat typography and assets as licensed infrastructure. Use immersive technology only when it expresses the subject, and retain semantic HTML plus mobile, reduced-motion, and no-WebGL fallbacks.

## Current delivered capabilities

The suite has four creative gears, broad site-archetype routing, evidence-led research, content and copy standards, art-direction guidance, font tooling, stack and integration guidance, motion and 3D practices, accessibility/performance/SEO gates, browser QA, evaluation prompts, Windows/macOS installers, and a deploy-ready showcase.

The showcase is a demonstration, not evidence of real clients or results. Replace its provisional contact address before public business launch.
