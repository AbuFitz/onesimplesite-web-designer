# Website archetype router

Read only the sections that match the project. Hybrids are normal; choose one primary archetype so the site has a dominant decision model.

## Local service and trades

Primary jobs: establish coverage, competence, response route, and low-friction contact. Prioritise service area, real work, named services, accreditation or insurance only when verified, process, reviews with provenance, and telephone or quote action. Mobile callers matter. Avoid luxury-agency language that hides the actual service.

## Professional practice

Primary jobs: explain fit and boundaries, build personal trust, and make the first conversation safe. Prioritise named expertise, scope, regulated status when verified, confidentiality, process, plain-language insight, and an appropriate disclaimer. Calm hierarchy usually outperforms spectacle.

## Hospitality, food, and place

Primary jobs: make the experience imaginable and remove practical uncertainty. Prioritise current photography, menu or offering, atmosphere, location, hours, accessibility, booking or directions, and live policies. Treat imagery and mobile reservations as product surfaces.

## Portfolio and creative studio

Primary jobs: show judgement, contribution, and fit. Projects need roles, constraints, process, outputs, and outcomes when supported. Avoid context-free mockup galleries. Let the work vary while the editorial system remains coherent.

## Campaign, event, or launch

Primary jobs: create one sharp premise and drive a time-bound action. Keep the narrative compressed. Use motion, countdowns, registration, or ticketing only when real and connected. Plan post-event or post-launch states.

## Editorial, publication, or knowledge site

Primary jobs: support scanning, reading, discovery, citation, and return visits. Design durable article, author, taxonomy, search, archive, media, and related-content patterns. Protect reading measure, hierarchy, and source provenance.

## Ecommerce frontend

Primary jobs: discovery, confidence, comparison, purchase, and post-purchase expectation. Product truth, imagery, variants, price, stock, delivery, returns, and checkout state outrank decoration. Do not fabricate products or claim a checkout works without platform integration. Design empty, loading, error, and unavailable states.

## SaaS or product marketing

Primary jobs: match a painful job to a credible product and reduce adoption risk. Use real interface evidence, integration truth, security/procurement information, pricing posture, and a consistent demo or trial path. Never build a fake dashboard merely to decorate the hero.

## Institutional, civic, education, or regulated

Primary jobs: task completion, trust, findability, accessibility, and governance. Use established design systems when mandated or materially helpful. Plain language, content ownership, search, document handling, service status, and WCAG conformance take priority over novelty.

## Immersive brand experience

Primary jobs: create attention and memory without trapping the visitor. One dominant interaction or scene should express the subject. Keep the offer, navigation, CTA, and fallback in semantic HTML. Budget loading and mobile behavior before scene polish.

## Multi-page system gate

When the site has many routes or editors, define content types and shared templates before styling every page. Inventory navigation, URL structure, filters, search, forms, permissions, legal pages, 404/500 states, and redirects. A beautiful homepage does not compensate for an incoherent system.
